Welcome

Hi, we're the Titans.

Hi! We are a FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) team based at the Illinois Mathematics and Science Academy in Aurora, Illinois. Titan Robotics #2022 was founded in 2006 and has since grown into our team of 50 students today. We strive to create a sustainable and diverse learning environment, empowering the future engineers of our generation. On top of building up our own students’ strength, we spread STEM education through outreach initiatives all across Illinois.

Our Work

What We Do

Compete

115 lbs · 3-Month Build Season

Every year, we compete in the annual FIRST Robotics Competition from January to March. In these three months, we CAD, build, wire, and program a fully-functional, 115 lb robot.

Community Impact

4,000+ Volunteer Hours

We give our time back to the communities that support us, volunteering at FIRST events and local programs all year long.

STEM Outreach

350+ STEM Kits Distributed

From SPIKE kit workshops to library visits, we bring hands-on STEM education to kids all across Illinois.

Mentorship

22 Cities · 10 Counties Reached

Experienced members teach rookies, and the whole team works alongside younger students discovering STEM for the first time.
