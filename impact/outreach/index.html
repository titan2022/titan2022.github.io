Impact → Outreach

Outreach

Outreach is a core part of Titan Robotics. We work with schools, libraries, community groups, and STEM organizations to make engineering and robotics more accessible while giving our members opportunities to teach, mentor, and lead.

STEM demonstrations

We bring robots and hands-on activities to schools, community events, and public spaces so students can see engineering in action.

Workshops & camps

We run workshops and seasonal programs that introduce students to robotics, programming, design, and the engineering process.

Mentorship

Team members mentor younger students and support other robotics programs by sharing technical knowledge and competition experience.

Community partnerships

Long-term partnerships with schools, libraries, nonprofits, and STEM organizations help us reach students across Illinois.

Why it matters

Building access and confidence

Our goal is not just to demonstrate a robot. We want students to leave outreach events believing that engineering is something they can do. By meeting students where they are and creating low-pressure, hands-on experiences, we help make STEM feel approachable.

Outreach also strengthens our own team. Teaching technical concepts forces us to understand them more deeply, while planning events gives members experience in communication, leadership, and project management.

Work with us

Interested in an outreach event?

If your school, library, organization, or community group is interested in working with Titan Robotics, contact us and tell us what you have in mind.
