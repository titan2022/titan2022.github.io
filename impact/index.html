Outreach

STEM beyond our lab

Our boarding school community brings students from across Illinois together in one place, and we use that reach to take STEM programming beyond campus. Through demonstrations, workshops, camps, and long-term partnerships, Titan Robotics works with students and organizations throughout the state.

Explore Our Outreach →

No-cost participation

Team participation is free. Sponsor funding and school support help remove cost as a barrier to joining FRC.

No experience required

Students can join without prior robotics experience and learn through hands-on work, mentorship, and peer teaching.

Statewide reach

Because IMSA is a residential school, our students bring perspectives and community connections from across Illinois.

Community partnerships

We collaborate with schools, libraries, STEM organizations, and local partners to make robotics and engineering more accessible.

Support the mission

Help keep FRC accessible

Sponsors make it possible for students to participate at no cost while supporting equipment, travel, outreach, and competition expenses.
