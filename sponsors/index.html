Why Sponsor Titan?

Every dollar goes to a student's opportunity

Robot materials

Without sponsorships that provide us with materials or monetary support, we wouldn't have been able to build competent robots year after year, especially since we pride ourselves on being completely zero-cost for our members.

Competition registration

Registering for competitions alone costs a large amount of money, meaning that thanks to our bigger partners, we're able to not only build a lot but also compete a lot as well.

Travel expenses

It costs a lot of money to transport members and robots alike, but thanks to sponsors, we are able to let our members experience the thrilling experience of attending events and competitions across the US.

Outreach supplies

With the help of sponsors, we've been able to connect with a much larger audience in the form of outreach events by providing them with innovative materials aimed at teaching a wide range of ages STEM skills such as coding and component vision.

Student opportunities

Operating within a school where student income can vary drastically, providing the opportunity to participate in robotics to all students was a top priority. So, we ask for no payment whenever a student is interested in joining, funding our team, materials, and travel expenses with 100% sponsorship donations.
