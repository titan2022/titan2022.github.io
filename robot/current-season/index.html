This season, we competed in the Central Illinois Regional and the Midwest Regional. Our primary strategy was to focus on supporting the strongest shooters on our alliance, shuttling fuel between the hub and the field to minimize our alliance's cycle time. In addition, we played defense when it was a strategically better option, closely following our opponent's strongest shooter and blocking the trench.
