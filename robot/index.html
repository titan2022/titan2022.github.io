Build Season

Design Process

  1. 01

    Brainstorming

    The team breaks down the new game and sketches out strategies and mechanism ideas together, making a needs-wants-don'ts list to guide priorities throughout the season.

  2. 02

    CAD

    Ideas become a full 3D model of the robot, where every part is designed and checked for fit before anything is built.

  3. 03

    Manufacturing

    Parts are machined and fabricated from the CAD drawings, turning stock material into real robot components.

  4. 04

    Assembly

    Subsystems come together as mechanisms are mounted, wired, and fitted onto the drivebase.

  5. 05

    Testing

    The robot runs on the practice field so drivers and programmers can find problems and fix them before competition.

  6. 06

    Competition

    The finished robot competes at official FRC events, with the whole team supporting from the pits and the stands.
