Leadership

Our team is led by students: three Captains, a Project Manager, and a lead for each of our seven subteams. The mentors who back them up all season are introduced further down this page.

Sofia

Senior Captain

Hey! I'm Sofia, and I'm a Senior. I started doing robotics in 2nd grade, and since then, it’s been a huge part of my life. Outside of robotics, I’m passionate about mental health awareness and technology in general. My favorite part about FIRST is the community it creates and how people come together in and out of the lab.

Olivia

Operations Captain

Hi! I’m Olivia, and I’m a Senior. Ever since eighth-grade FLL, I’ve loved everything about robotics. I’m also on an FTC team (shoutout Fast but not Furious #21592!). My favorite part about FIRST is the competitions because of the passion and energy that is on the field. Besides robotics, I love crocheting, watching shows, and talking with friends.

Amelia

Junior Captain

I’m Amelia, and I’m a Junior at Illinois Math and Science Academy! If I’m not in the lab or on Onshape, you can probably find me playing tennis or doing work for The Sex Ed Initiative. My favorite part of FIRST is how it helps driven people connect to solve problems.

Jack
