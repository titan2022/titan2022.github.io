Our Team

How we're organized

Seven subteams, one robot

Every student on Titan Robotics belongs to one of seven subteams. Four technical groups design, build, wire, and program the robot. Three operations groups handle outreach, relationships, and finance. Each group is student-led and works alongside the others throughout the season.

Mechanical

Fabricates, assembles, and maintains the robot’s physical systems from drivetrain to mechanisms.

Electrical

Builds and maintains the robot’s power, control, and sensor systems.

Design Integration

Coordinates CAD, prototyping, and subsystem integration so the robot comes together as one machine.

Programming

Develops robot software, autonomous routines, controls, and supporting technical tools.

Outreach

Plans STEM outreach, community events, and programs that extend the team’s impact beyond competition.

Relations

Manages team communications, media, branding, sponsor relationships, and public-facing work.

Finance

Builds the team budget, tracks spending, supports fundraising, and helps keep the program financially sustainable.

Student-led

Meet the people leading the work

Our Captains, Project Manager, and Subteam Leads coordinate the team across the full season.
