About Us

Titan Robotics is a no-cost, no experience needed team. Based at the Illinois Mathematics and Science Academy in Aurora, Illinois, we are made up of roughly 45 students in grades 10–12. Our unique boarding school environment creates a diverse team and allows us to perform significant outreach all across the state.

Our Mission

Titan Robotics #2022 empowers students to become innovative leaders through hands-on STEM, teamwork, and continuous learning while inspiring excellence, solving real-world challenges, and positively impacting our community.

We stand by the six FIRST core values of Discovery, Innovation, Impact, Inclusion, Teamwork, and Fun. By aligning our mission to FIRST, we have seen success demonstrated by our Awards in past seasons.
